How Grains Move Through the Turnkey Dal Processing Plants

01
Intake & Pre-Cleaning

Raw pulses pass through multi-purpose screeners and dry gravity de-stoners to remove stones, clay lumps, straw, and light chaff.

02
Pitting & Conditioning

Precision emery scratchers break the hard exterior hull followed by controlled water/oil tempering to loosen the seed coat without wetting the interior cotyledon.

03
High-Yield De-Husking

Our balanced Fatka machines and emery hullers strip the hulls efficiently with gentle, controlled friction, preventing grain shattering.

04
Aspiration & Husk Removal

Negative-pressure cyclones and rotary airlocks lift away detached husk into collection bins, leaving clean split and whole dal.

05
Buffing & Water Polishing

Buff polishers and dal polishers create a brilliant, natural shine and smooth texture demanded by commercial consumer markets.

06
Precision Sizing & Bagging

Multi-deck round graders and plansifters classify finished dal by size and split quality directly into clean packing bins.

How Grains Move Through the Modern Rice Milling & Polishing Plants

01
Paddy Pre-Cleaning

High-volume air aspiration strips light dust, immature paddy, and foreign straw before processing.

02
Gravity De-Stoning

A vibrating inclined deck separates heavy mud balls, stones, and glass fragments identical in size to paddy.

03
Gentle Rubber Roll Hulling

Hulling equipment removes husk from paddy; suitable machinery depends on the paddy and process requirements.

04
Brown Rice Separation

Paddy separators segregate unhusked paddy and return it for re-milling while clean brown rice advances.

05
Silky Water-Mist Polishing

Precision rice silky friction machines inject fine water mist to buff the bran layer into a brilliant translucent sheen.

06
Thickness & Length Grading

Round graders and plan swifters separate broken tips, broken halves, and oversized fractions into distinct outlets.

Dump Pit & Bulk Scalping

Rotary Drum Scalping

Separates fine dust and oversized lumps from finished grain streams.

02
Multi-Tier Gyratory Plansifting

Suspended plansifter cabin shakes nested sieve frames in a circular motion to divide fractions by aperture size.

Rotary Cylinder Sizing
