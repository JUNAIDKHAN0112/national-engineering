PLANNING A MACHINERY ENQUIRY

Details that help shape a useful enquiry

Machinery selection depends on the crop, process stages, required throughput and available site space. Include these details when contacting us.

01

Crop or product

Tell us whether you are processing pulses, rice, grains, seeds or another material.

02

Process stages

List the steps you need, such as cleaning, milling, grading, conveying or dust collection.

03

Expected throughput

Share your target output so the team can discuss suitable machine and plant options.

04

Site information

Provide your location and available floor space or building layout, if known.
